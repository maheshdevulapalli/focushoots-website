Book a Shoot
The process

From booking to a finished reel in about 10 minutes.

No back-and-forth, no editing software, no waiting days for a videographer. Here's exactly what happens when you book a Focushoot.

1

Open Focushoots and pick a creator

Browse trained iPhone creators near you, check their sample reels, and choose one based on availability and style. Most creators can be booked within the hour.

2

Tell us what you need

Pick a format — product reel, promo video, event recap, personal brand content — and share your location, timing, and any references. Takes under a minute.

3

Your Focushoots creator arrives and shoots

A trained creator shows up with just an iPhone and know-how — no bulky gear, no crew. They direct the shoot, capture multiple takes and angles, and keep it moving fast.

4

Edited and delivered in ~10 minutes

Footage is cut, color-graded, and set to music or voiceover on the spot. You get a ready-to-post reel or promo video directly to your phone — no waiting on a separate editor.

5

Post it

Download and publish straight to Instagram, YouTube Shorts, or wherever your audience is. That's it — content live, same session.

Why it's fast

Speed by design, not by shortcuts.

Every Focushoots creator is trained on a repeatable shoot-and-edit workflow built specifically for short-form content — so quality doesn't get traded for speed.

iPhone-first workflow

No lighting rigs or bulky cameras to set up — creators work with a proven, portable iPhone kit built for quick turnarounds.

Trained creators, not generalists

Every Focushoots creator is trained on framing, pacing, and editing specifically for reels — not general videography.

Edit on location

Editing happens right after the shoot, on the spot, so there's no queue, no handoff, and no waiting days for a draft.
